# Why Your Robot Vacuum Keeps Getting Stuck

Seven things cause 90% of stuck-ups. None of them require a new robot. Here's the list, in order of how often we see it in support tickets.

## The seven things that cause 90% of stuck-ups

### 1. Cable spaghetti under desks

The single most common cause. Phone chargers, lamp cords, and surge-protector trails wrap around the brush in seconds.

**Fix:** velcro cable loops or one of those fabric cord channels. Five minutes, $8.

### 2. Black or dark-pile rugs (cliff sensor false-positive)

The cliff sensor (the IR pair on the underside) reads dark surfaces as a missing floor and stops. Common on charcoal Persian rugs, dark berbers, and matte black tile.

**Fix:** in the app, mark the rug as a no-go zone, or use the "ignore cliff sensor" mode while the robot crosses (S-Series Pro and E-Series both support this).

### 3. 1.5cm threshold strips

The robot's max climb is usually 2.0cm. A 1.5cm strip with a beveled edge often bounces the front sensor instead of triggering climb mode.

**Fix:** flatten the bevel with a thin metal threshold ramp ($12 at any hardware store), or accept that the kitchen and the dining room are now two separate cleaning zones.

### 4. Bath mats with no rubber backing

The robot bunches the mat as it crosses, then the brush wraps the bunched fabric.

**Fix:** rubber-backed mats only. Or no-go zone the bathroom.

### 5. Chair legs in the under-3cm clearance zone

Some accent chairs have a low rail. The robot wedges between the rail and the floor.

**Fix:** move the chair, or add a no-go zone shaped around its base.

### 6. Old map data after furniture moves

When you move a couch, the old map's "open floor" is now blocked. The robot tries to drive through the couch and re-routes inefficiently.

**Fix:** in the app, run a re-mapping session. Takes 20 minutes. Don't try to edit the existing map — re-run.

### 7. Brush-roller hair binding

The roller starts spinning unevenly, the motor draws more current, and the safety cutout fires.

**Fix:** pop the roller, slice the hair off with the included tool. If it's the third time this month, the brush is past its life — replace it. The [6-month replenishment kit](/products/roborock-replenish-6mo) covers this.

## Quick fixes you can do in five minutes

1. Empty the dustbin (a full bin makes the suction motor work harder and trip the cutout).
2. Check the brush for hair wrap.
3. Wipe the cliff sensors with a dry cloth.
4. Confirm the map is current (re-map after any furniture move).
5. Restart the robot from the app, not the on-unit power button.

## When to retire the brush + filter

- **Brush**: 6 months in a pet household, 9–12 months otherwise. If it's stiff, frayed, or uneven — replace.
- **Filter**: 60–90 days. With pets: 30–45. If it looks gray and won't tap clean — replace.
- **Mop pads**: 2–3 months. Once they're stained, they're done.
