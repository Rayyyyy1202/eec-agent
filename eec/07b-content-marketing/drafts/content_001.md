## What "anti-tangle" actually means (and how to spot the marketing version)

Two design choices separate a real anti-tangle brush from a marketing-deck one:

1. **Geometry**: a V-shape (Roborock S-Series Pro) or a fully tangle-free comb design directs hair toward the dustbin port instead of wrapping the roller axle. A straight bristle roller — no matter how the brand brands it — wraps.
2. **Material**: silicone fins shed hair on the next pass. Bristle fingers grab and hold.

The 30-second visual test: pull the roller out. If it has a continuous bristle strip, it will wrap. If it's segmented silicone or a true open comb, it won't.

## What to ignore in pet-vacuum specs

- **"Pet edition" stickers**: usually means a green-and-black colorway and an extra brush in the box. Doesn't change the engineering.
- **HEPA-grade filter claims**: real HEPA H13 means 99.95% at 0.3 microns and is testable. Vague "HEPA-grade" or "HEPA-style" usually isn't.
- **Suction Pa numbers above 8,000**: marginal returns above 8,000 Pa for real-world floor cleaning. The brush geometry and seal matter more.

## FAQ

**Long-haired cats?** Same anti-tangle physics applies. The S-Series Pro V-brush handled 12 weeks of two long-haired cats in our second test apartment with no untangling.

**Dander allergens?** A real H13 filter (S-Series Pro has one) captures 99.95% at 0.3μm — that's the size most pet dander particles fall in. The E-Series uses a HEPA-grade filter that's not formally rated.

**When to replace the kit?** With a shedding pet, every 6 months is the realistic cadence. The [6-month replenishment kit](/products/roborock-replenish-6mo) is the boring-but-correct answer.
